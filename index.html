Lay Calc

Lay calculator, 2UP trade-out and odds matcher. Runs in your browser; your API key never leaves this device except to call The Odds API.

Lay an acca one leg at a time, each just before it kicks off, so you end on the same amount whatever happens. Enter the legs in kick-off order. When a leg wins, mark it Won, type the lay you actually placed, update the next leg's lay odds, and the rest recalculate. Stop as soon as a leg loses.

Your team has gone 2 goals up and the bookie has paid out. Back the team on the exchange to cancel the open lay and lock in profit, or let it ride.

Always re-check odds on the bookie and exchange before placing. Coverage: match result only.
